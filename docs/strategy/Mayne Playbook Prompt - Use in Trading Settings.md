# Scanner Evaluation Rules — Mayne OB/FVG System

## HTF Bias Rules
- Bullish bias: most recent decisive Market Structure Break (MSB/BOS) is to the upside — a prior swing high taken out with follow-through
- Bearish bias: most recent decisive MSB is to the downside — a prior swing low taken out with follow-through
- Ranging: no clear MSB in either direction; price oscillating between equal highs and lows

## Dealing Range Rules
- Draw from most recent significant swing high to most recent significant swing low
- "Significant" = visually obvious pivot; if debatable, it is not significant
- Equilibrium (EQ) = 50% midpoint of the range
- Discount zone = below EQ (look for longs here)
- Premium zone = above EQ (look for shorts here)
- Ideal entry zone is the 61.8–78.6% OTE region, not just barely above/below 50%
- A lick sweep (price briefly exceeds the high/low then immediately snaps back) does NOT reset the dealing range

## Order Block (OB) Quality Criteria
- A high-quality OB must satisfy ALL three: (1) swept a prior liquidity low (bullish) or high (bearish), (2) drove an explosive Market Structure Break, (3) printed its own FVG on the displacement away
- An OB that does not meet all three criteria is low-quality and should be scored down
- OB entry: price returns to the last opposing candle before the impulsive displacement
- A close through the full OB body (not just a wick) invalidates it

## Fair Value Gap (FVG) Quality Criteria
- A high-quality FVG requires: (1) massive, obvious displacement candles — not marginal imbalance, (2) occurred after an OB or MSB, (3) is unmitigated (price has not returned to it)
- FVGs in aggressive trending conditions are higher probability — price often leaves OBs untouched in fast trends
- FVGs in ranging conditions are lower probability — consecutive FVGs tend to overlap and fail
- A partially mitigated FVG (top touched but gap not filled) is lower quality than a virgin FVG

## Confluence Scoring
- OB + FVG + Breaker overlapping in the same zone = unicorn setup; highest priority, score up significantly
- OB + FVG stacked = high confluence
- OB alone (meets all 3 criteria) = solid setup
- FVG alone (large displacement, after MSB) = valid but lower priority than OB
- Ambiguous or low-quality POI = do not flag as active; score as watching or quiet

## Context Filter
- Aggressive trend: shallow pullbacks, fast momentum — prioritise FVG over OB (OB likely stays untouched)
- Ranging / sideways: choppy overlapping candles — prioritise OB over FVG (cleaner structural boundaries)
- Reversal at major HTF high or low: OB and Breaker Blocks strictly dominate; FVG entries low priority

## Valid Entry Triggers
- Order Block (OB) tap: price returns to the last opposing candle before an impulsive displacement
- Fair Value Gap (FVG) return: price retraces into a 3-candle imbalance zone created by a large displacement candle
- Liquidity Sweep + CHoCH: sell-side or buy-side liquidity swept followed by a Change of Character on the LTF
- Combined confluence: OB + FVG overlap in the same zone (highest probability)

## Setup Validity Requirements (ALL must be true for 'active' status)
- HTF bias is clear and unambiguous
- Price is in the correct zone (discount for longs, premium for shorts)
- At least one liquidity pool has been swept (internal or external)
- POI meets quality criteria — ambiguous setups must not be flagged active
- A valid entry trigger is present or printing on the LTF
- Risk:Reward minimum 2:1 to next external liquidity; prefer 3:1

## Invalidation Rules
- HTF bias shifts against the trade (new MSB in opposite direction)
- Price closes through the full order block body (not just a wick)
- Stop level taken out
- FVG aggressively traded through without an LTF structure shift — shift focus to the underlying OB
- OB aggressively traded through without an LTF structure shift — entire macro narrative invalidated

## Status Definitions
- active: HTF bias clear + price at high-quality POI + LTF entry trigger confirmed
- forming: HTF bias clear + price approaching POI + entry trigger not yet confirmed
- watching: HTF setup developing but missing 1–2 criteria (e.g. liquidity not yet swept, POI quality marginal)
- quiet: no meaningful setup; price in no-trade zone, bias unclear, or all POIs are low quality

## R:R Guidelines
- Minimum: 2:1 to next external liquidity
- Target: 3:1 or better
- Entry at POI, stop beyond the structure that invalidates, target at next external liquidity
- If FVG entry does not map to 2:1 minimum, skip FVG and wait for OB level
- If neither OB nor FVG produces 2:1, pass on the trade entirely
