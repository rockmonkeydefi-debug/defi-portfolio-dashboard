\## Fibonacci \& Optimal Trade Entry (OTE) Summary



\### 1. Core Concept



The Fibonacci retracement tool is a mathematical measuring instrument used to identify the \*\*Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)\*\*—the \*\*61.8% to 78.6% retracement zone\*\*.



\- \*\*Uptrend:\*\* Represents the deepest discount.

\- \*\*Downtrend:\*\* Represents the deepest premium.

\- \*\*High-Probability Setup:\*\* Achieved when the OTE zone aligns with a Point of Interest (POI) such as an order block, fair value gap, or breaker block.



\---



\### 2. Key Principles



\#### The OTE Zone Defined



\- \*\*The Bracket:\*\* 61.8% to 78.6% retracement of a price swing.

\- \*\*The Sweet Spot:\*\* \*\*70.5%\*\* is the single most precise level within the OTE zone.

\- \*\*Uptrend (Low-to-High):\*\* OTE resides in the discount zone (below the 50% Equilibrium). Hunt for longs.

\- \*\*Downtrend (High-to-Low):\*\* OTE resides in the premium zone (above the 50% Equilibrium). Hunt for shorts.



\#### How to Draw It



\- \*\*Bullish Setup:\*\* Anchor the tool at the significant swing low and drag to the significant swing high. Levels plot automatically below the high (in discount).

\- \*\*Bearish Setup:\*\* Anchor the tool at the significant swing high and drag to the significant swing low. Levels plot automatically above the low (in premium).

\- \*Note: Always use dealing range swing points.\*



\#### Invalidation Rules



\- \*\*Bullish Invalidation:\*\* A candle body close below the 78.6% level signals elevated probability that the uptrend is failing and the prior swing low will be taken out.

\- \*\*Bearish Invalidation:\*\* A candle body close above the 78.6% level signals elevated probability that the downtrend is failing and the prior swing high will be taken out.

\- \*\*Trend Strength Context:\*\* Strong trends produce shallow pullbacks. A near 80% retracement in a supposedly strong trend is a warning sign, not an isolated buying opportunity.



\---



\### 3. Probability Framework



| Factor | High-Probability Setup | Low-Probability / Proceed with Caution |

|---|---|---|

| \*\*Retracement Depth\*\* | Price pulls into OTE (61.8%–78.6%) | Price only reaches 23.6% or 38.2% |

| \*\*PD Array Alignment\*\* | OB, FVG, or breaker block stacked within OTE zone | POIs exist but sit above 50% EQ (in a bullish move) |

| \*\*Confluence Count\*\* | Multiple POIs overlap in OTE (e.g., OB + FVG + breaker) | Single POI with no OTE confirmation |

| \*\*Candle Behavior\*\* | Wick into OTE, body closes above 78.6% (longs) or below 78.6% (shorts) | Body closes below 78.6% (longs) or above 78.6% (shorts) |

| \*\*Timeframe\*\* | 4H chart and above | Sub-4H (lower reliability) |

| \*\*Market Condition\*\* | Clear trending structure with defined swing low/high | Ranging / consolidating market |



\---



\### 4. Execution Checklist



\- \[ ] \*\*Establish HTF Bias:\*\* Determine whether you are hunting longs (uptrend) or shorts (downtrend) based on market structure.

\- \[ ] \*\*Identify Dealing Range:\*\* Mark the most recent significant swing low and swing high.

\- \[ ] \*\*Draw Fibonacci Retracement:\*\* Low-to-high for bullish; high-to-low for bearish. Confirm 0.5, 0.618, 0.705, and 0.786 levels plot correctly.

\- \[ ] \*\*Mark the OTE Zone:\*\* Highlight the 61.8%–78.6% band on the chart as the primary area of interest.

\- \[ ] \*\*Identify POIs Within OTE:\*\* Look for order blocks, fair value gaps, or breaker blocks falling inside the zone. Prioritize setups where two or more overlap.

\- \[ ] \*\*Monitor Price Entry:\*\* Wait for price to trade into the zone; do not anticipate or front-run.

\- \[ ] \*\*Select Entry Execution Strategy:\*\*

&#x20; - \*\*Option A (HTF Entry):\*\* Enter directly at the POI within OTE. Place the stop loss below the swing low (bullish) or above the swing high (bearish).

&#x20; - \*\*Option B (LTF Confirmation Entry):\*\* Drop to a lower timeframe. Wait for a clear Market Structure Shift (MSS/MSB) as price interacts with the HTF OTE zone. Enter on the LTF reversal for a tighter stop and improved R:R.

\- \[ ] \*\*Monitor Candle Closes:\*\* If a candle body closes through the 78.6% level against the trend direction, invalidate the setup and reassess.

\- \[ ] \*\*Enforce Discipline:\*\* Do not enter a bare OTE zone that lacks confluent POIs. No POI confluence = no trade signal.
