# **Consolidated Trading Plan**

## **Fundamentals**

## **Determine High Timeframe (HTF) Bias**

Before analyzing local price action, identify the "macro direction" to ensure you are trading with the overall trend.

* **The Hierarchy:** Use a top-down approach.   
  * Weekly: Macro direction (where has price been for weeks/months?)  
  * Daily: Major swings, weeks of trend data  
  * H4: Local trend, last few days to 2 weeks  
  * H1 / M15 / M5: Entry timeframes  
* **Identify the Trend:** Look for Market Structure Breaks (MSB) on the Weekly and Daily charts.  
* **Directional Filter:** If the Weekly and Daily are bearish, only look for short setups on lower timeframes, even if the 15-minute chart appears bullish.  
    
  **Map the Dealing Range**  
  Within your biased timeframe, identify the boundaries of the most recent significant price leg.  
* **Identify Boundaries:** Mark the significant Swing High (highest point before a pullback) and Swing Low (lowest point before a bounce).  
  * "Significant" \= clear and visually obvious; if you have to debate it, it's not significant.  
  * Swing high \= highest point traded before a meaningful pullback.  
  * Swing low \= lowest point traded before a meaningful bounce.  
  * Recency matters — use the most recent significant pivots.  
* **Equilibrium:** Mark the 50% line to separate the range.  
* **Premium Zone:** The area above the 50% line (look for shorts here).  
* **Discount Zone:** The area below the 50% line (look for longs here).  
    
* **Dealing Range Formation**: Every time price makes a market structure break (MSB), a new dealing range forms.  
  * A "lick sweep" (price briefly trades beyond the high/low then snaps back) does NOT reset the range


  ## **Locate Liquidity & Entry Zones**

  Identify where price is going.

* **Internal Range Liquidity:** Inside the range; Used for Entries  
  * Order blocks, fair value gaps, swing highs/lows within the range.  
* **External Range Liquidity**: Outside the range boundaries (Highs/Lows); Used for Targets  
* **The Liquidity Cycle** (repeats endlessly, all timeframes, all assets)  
  * Internal → External → New Range → Internal → External → Repeat

## **Pre-Entry Checklist**

Confirm these items before executing any trade:

* **HTF Market Structure**  
  * Is the overall HTF structure bullish or bearish? (weekly/daily)  
  * Where was the most recent market structure break \- upside or downside?  
    * This dictates trading direction  
* **Dealing Range (DR)**  
  * What’s the HTF current range and where is price in that range?   
    * Are we in the DISCOUNT (below EQ)?  → Acceptable for longs  
    * Are we in the PREMIUM (above EQ)?  → Acceptable for shorts  
    * Are we at the EQ? → no action   
  * Within each TF, where are we in the DR?  
    * If HTF is bullish but price is in the premium → wait. Do not long \- the pullback will come. The discount is where the higher low forms.  
  * Confluence \- Ideal Scenario Example  
    * Weekly, Daily and H4 are all Bullish and price is in the Discount area  
    * This lines up for a long trade as all TFs are Bullish and in the Discount  
    * This works for any HTF → LTF analysis  
* **Liquidity**   
  * What liquidity was JUST taken out?  
    * Internal or External liquidity?  
    * Buy side or sell side?  
  * What is the NEXT most likely liquidity target?  
    * If we just took internal → next target is external.  
    * If we just took external → next target is internal (new range forms).  
    * For a long: target should be buy side / external above the market.  
    * For a short: target should be sell side / external below the market.  
  * For trade targets, is there a clear external target?  
    * Can you point to a specific target on the chart and explain WHY price is going there?  
    * If not → there is no trade  
* **Risk/Reward**  
  * Does the potential profit justify the risk?  
  * 2:1 minimum; 3:1 is better  
  * Enter on a lower TF for a better entry/tighter stop loss  
* **Entry Model:**   
  * Enter at the identified internal liquidity point and set your target at the external range liquidity on the opposite side.

## **Step By Step Process**

* Step 1: Weekly chart  
  * Identify the trend: bullish or bearish?  
  * Draw the current dealing range (swing low to swing high)  
  * Mark premium, discount, and EQ (use Fib tool)  
  * Identify BSL and SSL  
  * Note what liquidity was recently taken and what's next.  
* Step 2: Daily chart  
  * Is the daily aligned with the weekly bias?  
  * If daily appears contrary (e.g., daily looks bearish in a weekly uptrend), determine: is this a reversal or a pullback into the discount?  
  * Are we in the discount of the daily dealing range?  
  * Has internal range liquidity been recently swept?  
  * What is the next daily target (ERL)?  
* Step 3: H4 chart  
  * Confirm local trend aligns with weekly/daily.  
  * Draw the H4 dealing range.  
  * Are we in the discount (for longs) or premium (for shorts)?  
* Step 4: Entry timeframe (H1 / M15)  
  * Paradise TF Entry Hierarchy: W → H4; D → H1; H4 → M15; H1 → M5  
  * All checklist items confirmed?  
  * Identify POIs (OB, FVG)  
  * Entry model present? (clean setup)  
  * Risk-reward ≥ 2:1?  
  * Stop placed correctly?  
  * Execute

## **Trade Management** 

* TARGET external range liquidity, not internal.  
* Partial profits at internal range liquidity are acceptable; trail stop, hold core.  
* Do NOT close the full position at internal reaction — it's a stepping stone.  
* If HTF bias shifts (market structure break against the trade), reassess immediately.  
* Zoom into a lower TF dealing range for tighter stops and better RR when possible.

**Discipline Reminders**

* If checklist items are not ticked → NO TRADE. Zero exceptions.  
* Taking fewer trades is a feature; filters out bad trades. Quality over quantity.  
* Repeat the full analysis at the start of every week (or every session if scalping).  
* If there’s a HTF bias shift, restart the entire process from the weekly.  
* If the setup looks good but you can't identify WHERE price is going → no setup.

**Price Action Tips**

* Big wicks indicate weakness


    
    
  


## **References**

   Dealing Range: Most recent significant swing high to swing low (or vice versa).  
   EQ / Equilibrium: The 50% midpoint of the dealing range.  
   Premium: Above EQ; optimal area to SHORT.  
   Discount: Below EQ; optimal area to LONG.  
   BSL / Buy Side Liquidity: Liquidity above swing highs and range highs.  
   SSL / Sell Side Liquidity: Liquidity below swing lows and range lows.  
   IRL: Internal Range Liquidity; inside the current range; used for entries.  
   ERL: External Range Liquidity; outside the current range; the trade target.  
   MSB: Market Structure Break; when price takes out a prior significant high/low in the same direction as the current trend.  
   MSS: Market Structure Shift; reversal signal (lower high/higher low formed).  
   SFP: Swing Failure Pattern; lick sweep with no real displacement; range stays.  
   HTF Bias: The directional conclusion from the highest timeframe analysis.
